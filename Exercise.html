Grade-11 – CHEMISTRY – CHAPTER-6 – Nitrogen and its Behaviours

1.Metals are good conductors of head and electricity.

2.Non-metals are located on the left-side of the periodic Table.

3.Nitrogen is present to the extent of about 21 percentage by volume of the air.

4.Nitrogen gas is relatively inactive and does not support combustion.

5.Ammonia gas is collected by the upward displacement of air.
